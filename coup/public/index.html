    Cheat Sheet

    Base Actions (any player, any time — no character needed)

    Income +1 coin
    Take 1 coin. Cannot be blocked or challenged.
    Foreign Aid +2 coins
    Take 2 coins. Anyone can block by claiming Duke.
    Coup −7 coins
    Target loses an influence. Unblockable, unchallengeable. Mandatory at 10+ coins.

    Characters (claim one — anyone can bluff any card)

    ♦ Duke
    Tax: +3 coins.
    Blocks: Foreign Aid.
    ♠ Assassin
    Assassinate: pay 3 coins, target loses an influence. Blockable by Contessa.
    ♣ Captain
    Steal: take 2 coins from a target.
    Blocks: Stealing.
    ♥ Ambassador
    Exchange: draw 2 cards, return 2 to the deck.
    Blocks: Stealing.
    ★ Contessa
    Blocks: Assassination. No action of her own.

    Challenges & Blocks

    Challenge: call someone's bluff on a claimed character. If they have it, you lose an influence (they reveal, shuffle it back, and draw a new card). If they don't, they lose an influence and the action fails.

    Block: stop an action by claiming a character that counters it. A block can itself be challenged — if the blocker can't prove it, the original action goes through.

    Losing influence: reveal one of your face-down cards, face up, forever. Lose your last one and you're eliminated. Last player standing wins.
